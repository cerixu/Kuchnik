# Food illustration style standard

The canonical visual references are the user-approved files:
- `assets/food-svg/carbonara.svg`
- `assets/food-svg/bruschetta.svg`

All future recipe artwork must match their painterly, richly detailed food rendering, realistic ingredient textures, dark plate/table presentation, lighting, composition, and polished restaurant-menu finish. Do not use flat iconography, geometric cartoon shapes, emoji-like artwork, or simplified hand-drawn SVG illustrations.

Before adding a new dish illustration, compare it side by side against both references. Keep the dish identity accurate and distinct. Use local assets and preserve offline availability. User-uploaded photos always take precedence. Never replace or restyle the two approved references without explicit user approval.
